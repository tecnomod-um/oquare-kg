# The OQuaRE KG quality characteristics

The OQuaRE KG framework includes 5 characteristics: structural, functional adequacy, compatibility, transferability and operability.

## Structural
The structural dimension accounts for the intrinsic design and structure of the knowledge graph, independent of the user's specific context. 

## Functional adequacy
Functional adequacy refers to the capability of the knowledge graph to provide concrete functions. [^1]

## Compatibility
The ability of a knowledge graph to function correctly and be integrated with other knowledge graphs, data sources, or systems by adhering to common technical standards for data representation and exchange.

## Transferability
The degree to which a knowledge graph can be deployed, reused, or adapted across different platforms, domains, or technical environments with minimal modification, preserving its data, schema, and semantic definitions.

## Operability
The level of effort required by users (e.g., developers, data scientists, domain experts) to effectively access, query, navigate, and update the information stored within the knowledge graph to perform their tasks.

## Reliability
Reliability refers to the capability of the knowledge graph to maintain its level of performance under stated conditions for a given period of time.[^1]

[^1]:Duque-Ramos, A., Fernández-Breis, J. T., Stevens, R., & Aussenac-Gilles, N. (2011). OQuaRE: A SQuaRE-based Approach for Evaluating the Quality of Ontologies. Journal of Research and Practice in Information Technology, 43(2).
